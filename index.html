How the demo works

Four steps, all in the browser.

STEP 1

Pick a package

Six starting amounts from ₹500 to ₹20,000, each with a preset 14-day illustrative reward schedule.

STEP 2

Add demo funds

A simulated payment screen with UPI/QR, card, net banking and wallet. No gateway is contacted.

STEP 3

Run the days

Advance simulated days one by one or use accelerated playback, and watch the balance move.

STEP 4

Track the portfolio

Growth chart, demo ledger and a simulated withdrawal — all stored in your browser only.
