5-Point Agenda
CJP Ka Manifesto 🪳
Satire se shuru hua, lekin demands bilkul real hain. #CJP2029
DEMAND 01
📋
RTI Full Accountability
Har government body RTI ke under aaye. Transparency sirf slogan nahi, practice bane. Cockroaches demand transparency!
DEMAND 02
⚖️
No Rajya Sabha for Retired CJI
Retire hone ke baad Chief Justice ko Rajya Sabha seat nahi milni chahiye. Post-retirement "reward" band karo.
DEMAND 03
👩‍💼
50% Women in Parliament
Parliament aur Cabinet mein 50% reservation for women — without increasing the total strength of the house.
DEMAND 04
🚫
20-Year Ban for Defectors
Jo MP ya MLA party tod ke doosri party mein jaye — unhe 20 saal ke liye public office se ban karo. Dal-badlu neta nahi chalega!
DEMAND 05
💰
No Anonymous Donations
No electoral bonds. No secret funds. Zero anonymous political donations. Paise ka hisaab do — public ko jaanne ka haq hai!
BONUS
📝
CBSE Recheck Fees Band Karo
Recheck fees "blatant corruption" hai. Students se paisa mat looto. NEET controversy pe bhi CJP ka full support!
