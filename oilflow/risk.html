Overview / Risk

Risk and disclosure

Read this before drawing conclusions from anything else in the project.

IMPORTANT

This is a simulation

OILFLOW does not accept deposits, hold funds, execute trades or facilitate any financial transaction. Every balance, reward, bonus and withdrawal in the project is a fictitious number generated in the browser.

No guarantees of any kind

The preset reward schedules are deliberately exaggerated to demonstrate an interface. They are not promises of profit, fixed income or capital protection, and they are not forecasts of what any asset will do.

Commodity risk is real

Actual crude-oil prices can fall sharply and stay low for extended periods. Where leverage is involved, losses can equal or exceed the amount invested. Nothing in this simulator reflects those mechanics.

Not financial advice

Nothing here is a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any asset. Speak to a licensed professional before making real investment decisions.

Synthetic data and mock payments

Price series are generated algorithmically and are not derived from any exchange. The Add Funds screen has no payment gateway, no UPI integration, no bank connection and no card processing. Its QR code resolves to no payment address, so scanning it cannot create a payment.

Educational purpose

The project exists to explain the relationship between price movement, exposure and profit or loss — and to demonstrate a product interface. It is not a product.

ⓘ   If this project were ever turned into a real service in India, it would need a registered operating entity, an authorised partner for any actual commodity product, verified payment processing, KYC and grievance handling, and legal review. None of that exists here.
