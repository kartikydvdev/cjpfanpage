What moves oil? (educational)

LEARN
Supply decisions

Production quotas from major producers shift the supply curve.

Demand cycles

Industrial activity, travel seasons and broader economic growth.

Inventories

Weekly stockpile reports signal surplus or tightness.

Currency and rates

Oil is priced in USD, so dollar strength affects global demand.

How it links to the demo

EXPLAINED
OIL PRICE MOVEMENT ↓ SIMULATION ENGINE ↓ PACKAGE VALUE ↓ DEMO REWARD
In market-linked demo mode the daily figure is simply the starting amount multiplied by a daily movement you choose — an arithmetic illustration, not a market return. In fixed mode the schedule ignores oil prices entirely. Neither mode connects to a real commodity market.
